AutoClip Business

Local software for the automated processing and publishing of authorized video content.

What it does

AutoClip Business runs on the owner's own computer. It analyzes video files the owner is authorized to use, cuts short vertical clips, adds subtitles, runs quality and rights checks, and uploads the finished clips to the owner's own YouTube channel. A clip is only processed or published if a documented permission for that source exists; without proof of rights, nothing is published.

Use of Google OAuth and YouTube access

The application accesses YouTube only after the account owner has given explicit consent in Google's official sign-in and consent screen. It requests two permissions:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload – to upload videos to the channel the owner selected.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly – to read the channel's name and ID (to confirm the correct channel) and the processing and privacy status of videos the application uploaded.

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account permissions page. Details on what is stored and how are in the Privacy Policy.

AutoClip Business's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
